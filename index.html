10 Things You Didn't Know About Crocodiles

  • Dinosaur Survivors: They have existed for 240 million years.
  • Bone Crushers: They have the strongest bite force on Earth.
  • Night Hunters: Their eyes glow to see in total darkness.
  • The Death Roll: They spin to take down large prey.
  • Stone Swallowers: Stones in their stomach help them dive.
  • Tail Power: They swim as fast as motorboats.
  • Nest Secrets: Nest heat determines if babies are boys or girls.
  • Smart Reptiles: They can remember human faces.
  • Mouth Gaping: They open their mouths to stay cool.
  • Natural Armor: Their skin is reinforced with bone plates.
